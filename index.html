Archive compatibility

Downloads use AES-encrypted ZIP archives. If your device's built-in unzip tool does not support AES archives, use an AES-compatible extractor such as 7-Zip on Windows or Keka on macOS.

WHY AGOTHEOUTPUT EXISTS

Not a giant marketplace.
A small output engine.

AgotheOutput is the storefront surface of an autonomous manufacturing workflow. Products are generated, checked, packaged and routed through a repeatable release system. Some products may use AI-assisted or procedural computational design; applicable product materials disclose that clearly.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Are these physical products?

No. Every launch product is a digital download. Nothing is shipped.

Do I need an AgotheOutput account?

No. Checkout is handled by Stripe and currently only requires the information Stripe requests for the order.

Why is the download encrypted?

The public file host stores an encrypted archive. The archive password is delivered only through the successful post-checkout route.

Can I trademark one of the logo marks?

The logo starter kit is sold as non-exclusive premade artwork. Trademark clearance and exclusivity are not included.
